Terms of Service

These terms describe the boundaries for using Incident Postmortem Checker as a read-only extraction and completeness checking service.

Service description

The service performs read-only extraction of explicitly stated postmortem information and checks supplied material against user-provided completeness requirements.

Allowed use

You may use the app to organize and check incident postmortem material that you are authorized to provide.

User responsibility

  • Ensure you are authorized to submit the material.
  • Review output accuracy before relying on it.
  • Perform human verification before taking action.
  • Avoid submitting unnecessary passwords, secret keys, or sensitive credentials.

Limitations

Outputs depend on the supplied material and may be incomplete, ambiguous, or incorrect.

No external execution

  • Does not modify incident systems.
  • Does not assign tasks.
  • Does not contact people.
  • Does not run remediation commands.
  • Does not execute action items.

No professional advice

Output is not legal, security, compliance, or other professional advice.

No destructive actions

The app has no capability to delete, overwrite, or damage external data.

No guarantees

The app does not guarantee that input material is true, output is perfectly accurate, or a postmortem meets a specific organization's internal standards.

Prohibited use

  • Submitting content you are not authorized to process.
  • Sending passwords, API keys, access tokens, or other credentials.
  • Abusing the service.
  • Attempting to bypass security limits.
  • Presenting output as verified real-world fact.

Changes to service

The service and these terms may be updated over time.
